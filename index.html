Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from brands and partners

Which regions do you currently cover? +

Our distribution vision is pan-India, with active expansion into new states and cities on a structured basis, alongside select international markets including Qatar, UAE, Sri Lanka, Canada and the USA.

What product categories do you work with? +

We work exclusively within Food & Beverage — including packaged foods, snacks, bakery, confectionery, ready-to-eat and ready-to-cook products, spices, beverages, tea & coffee, and more — subject to applicable regulatory requirements.

How do I become a distributor or dealer partner? +

Reach out through our contact form with your business details and region of interest. Our team will get in touch to discuss appointment as a channel partner.

Do you work with international F&B brands? +

Yes. We aim to serve as a market-development and distribution partner for international brands entering India, subject to applicable Indian laws, food-safety regulations, import requirements and labeling norms.

How long does onboarding typically take? +

Timelines vary by brand and target region. After initial onboarding and market mapping, we work towards distributor appointment and retail activation in a structured, phased manner.
